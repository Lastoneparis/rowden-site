Rowden Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 July 2026

Rowden is an unofficial mobile client for your own Postgres / Supabase projects. We host nothing and we do not collect, store, or transmit your data to our own servers. Rowden is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Supabase.

What stays on your device

  • Project URL & API keys — stored only in your device Keychain and used to connect directly to your own project.
  • Tables, rows, users, storage and query results — fetched directly from your project to your device. Rowden never sees them.

Data we collect

None. No analytics servers, no account system.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple. We receive no payment information.
