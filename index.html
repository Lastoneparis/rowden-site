Rowden

Your Postgres, honestly in your pocket.

Rowden connects straight to your own Postgres or Supabase project — browse live tables, filter and sort them, edit rows, then upload files, export CSV and run SQL. On iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Vision Pro.

What you can do

  • Live tables and rows from your project, with your own keys — filter and sort server-side via PostgREST.
  • Type-aware data grid: boolean badges, explicit NULL, formatted dates, right-aligned numbers.
  • Edit and insert rows inline, and run SQL through RPC in a full editor (Premium).
  • View auth users and analytics KPIs, and upload files to storage buckets (Premium).
  • A clearly labeled, read-only Demo Project to try everything with no setup.

Secure by design

Your keys stay in the device Keychain. Rowden talks straight to your project's API — no middle server, and no telemetry on your data.
